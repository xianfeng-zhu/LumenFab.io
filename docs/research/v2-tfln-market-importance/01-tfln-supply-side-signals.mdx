## 5. TSMC COUPE (Taiwan)

TSMC's silicon photonics integration platform using SoIC-X (3D stacking of EIC over PIC).

| Phase | Year | Capability | Status |
|-------|------|------------|--------|
| Phase 1 | 2025 | 1.6 Tbps (OSFP) | In production |
| Phase 2 | 2026 | 6.4 Tbps (CoWoS CPO) | Mass production |
| Phase 3 | TBD | 12.8 Tbps | In development |

### TFLN / Micro-Ring Modulator Integration

- TSMC started production of a **200 Gbps micro-ring modulator** using COUPE in 2025 (BER < 1e-8).
- COUPE PDK includes: grating couplers, edge couplers, SiN waveguides, Ge photodetectors, and micro-ring modulators.
- **No explicit TFLN module** found in public COUPE documentation.
- Standard COUPE process: **65 nm, 12-inch wafers**, 6 metal layers, waveguide control to 2nm accuracy.

### Ecosystem

- EDA partnerships: Ansys, Synopsys, Cadence.
- Adopters: NVIDIA, Broadcom.
- Key supply chain: FOCI, Shunsin Technology, Xintec, MSSCorps, Nextronics.
- 200+ person R&D team on silicon photonics.

**Source**: TSMC press, SDxCentral, Tencent Cloud, industry analyst reports.

## 6. Tower Semiconductor PH18 (Israel)

### Key Finding: No TFLN Module

Tower Semiconductor does **not** currently offer a TFLN module on its PH18 platform. Instead, Tower is pursuing a different materials strategy:

| Development | Date | Technology |
|-------------|------|------------|
| Xscape multi-wavelength laser | Aug 2025 | Optically pumped, on-chip MWL on PH18 |
| Xanadu quantum photonics | Feb 2026 | SiN waveguides + photodiodes on PH18 |
| Lightwave Logic EO polymer modulators | Mar 2026 | 110GHz+, 400G/lane, multiple engineering tapeouts in 2026 |

**Conclusion**: Tower's PH18 is a mature 200mm SiPho foundry platform, but TFLN integration is **not** on its public roadmap. Their bet is on EO polymers, not TFLN, for high-speed modulation.

**Source**: Nasdaq press release (Mar 2026), SimplyWallSt, Quantum Computing Report.

## Summary: The Supply-Side Story in Three Points

1. **Capacity is exploding**: From ~300K TFLN modulators/year in 2025 to projected multi-million/year by 2028. Foundries are adding capacity simultaneously in Switzerland (CCRAFT, Lightium), Europe (LIGENTEC+X-FAB), Taiwan (HyperLight+UMC), and China (AFR, Nanzhi).

2. **Major foundries are committing**: UMC (global top-3 foundry) licensing imec SiPho and partnering on TFLN, with its own photonics platform planned for 2027. X-FAB (Europe's largest analog foundry) partnering with LIGENTEC for TFLN-on-SiN/SOI volume production.

3. **But Tower PH18 is NOT TFLN**: Tower is betting on EO polymers (via Lightwave Logic) rather than TFLN for high-speed modulation — a divergent strategic choice worth tracking.

### Key Open Questions

- Can UMC's 8-inch TFLN ramp deliver by Q3/Q4 2026 as targeted?
- Will NVIDIA select TFLN for Rubin Ultra NVL576 CPO (decision expected H2 2027)?
- How fast can 8-inch LNOI substrate supply scale to meet foundry demand?
- When will IEEE 802.3dj 400G/lane standard finalize (2026-2027)?
- Can Chinese TFLN players access Western markets given geopolitical tensions?
