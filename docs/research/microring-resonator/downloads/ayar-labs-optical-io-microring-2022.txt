# Ayar Labs: Optical I/O for Next-Gen Computing
Source: https://www.optica-opn.org/home/industry/2022/may/ayar_labs_bets_on_optical_i_o_for_next-gen_computi/
Publisher: Optica / Optics & Photonics News (industry article)
Date accessed: 2026-06-18

## Summary
Ayar Labs develops CMOS-friendly, low-power optical I/O chiplets that place electrical-to-optical / optical-to-electrical conversion right next to a host ASIC (e.g., Nvidia GPU). The basic building block is a system of microring resonators.

## Product Architecture
- TeraPHY: optical I/O chiplet flip-chip bonded next to the host ASIC.
- SuperNova: external multi-wavelength laser light source that feeds TeraPHY.
- Single-mode fiber ribbon carries data-carrying light into/out of the chiplet.
- Microring resonators decode/encode the optical signal.

## Why Microrings Matter Here
- Miniaturization and integration enabled by microring-resonator architecture allows O/E and E/O conversion at much higher density and lower energy than traditional pluggable transceivers.
- Converts high-bandwidth electrical data from the host SoC to the optical domain quickly, then moves massive bandwidth over long distances with low energy.

## Real-World Context
- Partnerships with Nvidia, HPE, Intel, GlobalFoundries.
- Early units shipping to customers; roadmap toward high-volume parts.

## Takeaway for Page
Shows microring resonators as a commercial building block for co-packaged optics and chip-to-chip optical interconnects in AI/HPC systems.
