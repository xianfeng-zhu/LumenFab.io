# Free spectral range
Source: https://www.rp-photonics.com/free_spectral_range.html
Author: Dr. Rüdiger Paschotta (RP Photonics)
Date accessed: 2026-06-18

## Definition
Frequency spacing of the axial (longitudinal) modes of an optical resonator. Also called axial/longitudinal mode spacing.

## Formulas
- Empty standing-wave resonator of length L:
  Δν = c / (2L)
- Ring resonator: factor of 2 removed (no double pass):
  Δν = c / L  (empty)
- Standing-wave resonator filled with dispersive medium:
  Δν = c / (2 ng L)
  where ng is the group index.
- Waveguide resonator: calculate group index using frequency-dependent effective refractive index.
- More generally, FSR = inverse of round-trip group delay.

## Practical Relevance
- In a Fabry–Pérot interferometer, FSR limits the optical frequency range usable as a spectrometer.
- A large FSR is obtained by making the resonator shorter, but this also increases resonance bandwidth (lower spectral resolution) unless finesse is increased by reducing losses.
- For tunable single-frequency lasers, FSR often limits the mode-hop-free tuning range.
