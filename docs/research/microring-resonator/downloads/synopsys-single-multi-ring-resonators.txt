## Summary
A ring resonator is an important component in silicon photonics. It is made up of an optical waveguide in a loop fashion with a coupling mechanism.

## Resonance Wavelength
λ_res = (neff L) / m
where m is a non-zero positive integer, L is the optical round-trip length of the ring, and neff is the effective index of the waveguide.

## Free Spectral Range
FSR = λ² / (ng L)
Group refractive index can be used for more accurate values.

## Finesse
Finesse = FSR / FWHM
where FWHM is the full-width-half-maximum of a resonance.

## Quality Factor
Q = resonance frequency / FWHM
