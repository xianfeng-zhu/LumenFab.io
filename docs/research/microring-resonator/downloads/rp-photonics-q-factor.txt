# Q-factor
Source: https://www.rp-photonics.com/q_factor.html
Author: Dr. Rüdiger Paschotta (RP Photonics)
Date accessed: 2026-06-18

## Definition
A measure of the damping of resonator modes. Dimensionless.

## Common Definitions
1. Energy storage: Q = 2π × (stored energy) / (energy dissipated per oscillation cycle)
2. Resonance bandwidth: Q = ν0 / δν
   where ν0 is resonance frequency and δν is FWHM bandwidth.

## Optical Resonator Formula (low-loss limit)
Q = (2π ν0 T_rt) / l
where ν0 is optical frequency, T_rt is round-trip time, and l is fractional power loss per round trip (l ≪ 1).

## Important Relations
- Q = 2π × (energy decay time) × optical frequency
- Q = 2π × (number of oscillation periods for energy to decay to 1/e)
- Q = finesse × (optical frequency / FSR)

## Intrinsic vs Loaded Q
- Intrinsic Q: determined only by inherent resonator losses.
- Loaded Q: lower; includes losses from intentional external coupling.
- 1/Q_loaded = 1/Q_intrinsic + coupling term

## High-Q Resonators
- Supermirror cavities: Q > 10^11
- Silica toroidal microcavities: Q > 10^8
- Silica microspheres (whispering-gallery modes): Q ≈ 10^10

## Applications
- Telecommunications filters
- Optical frequency standards / narrow-linewidth lasers
- Quantum optics
