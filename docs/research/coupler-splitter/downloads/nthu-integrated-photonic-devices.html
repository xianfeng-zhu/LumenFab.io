Integrated Photonic Devices

課程說明：介紹積體光電元件及理論，介紹其在光通訊、光積體電路的應用

Content：

1. Introduction to Integrated Photonic Devices

2. Background of Electromagnetic Wave Optics

3. Theory of Optical Waveguide

4.  Waveguide Dispersion

5. Waveguide Loss

6. Waveguide Fabrication

7. Waveguide Couplers and Coupled Mode Theory

8. MZI, MMI and AWG

9. Waveguide I/O Couplers

10. Integrated Optical Resonator (I)

11. Integrated Optical Resonator (II)

12. Integrated Isolators

13. Integrated EO Modulators
14. Integrated AO Modulators
15. Introduction to Optoelectronics
16. Integrated Photodetectors
17. Light Emission in Semiconductors
18. Semiconductor Lasers
19. Heterostructure Lasers

20. DFB Lasers

 

Text Book：

"Integrated Optics - Theory and Technology", by R. G. Hunsperger, 5th ed., Springer Verlag.

Reference Books：

1. "Fundamentals of Optical Waveguides", by Katsunari Okamoto, Academic Press

2. "Integrated Photonics" by C. R. Pollock, Michal Lipson, Clifford Pollock, Kluwer Academic Pub
3. "Fundamentals of Photonics," by Saleh and Teich, Wiley Interscience.
