**Source:** Nature Reviews Physics, Volume 7, Pages 237-254 (2025)

## Abstract Summary

Comprehensive review covering fundamental principles of electro-optics and recent advances in thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN) photonics. TFLN offers strong electro-optic coupling, ultralow optical loss, high microwave bandwidth, tight field confinement, and compatibility with nanofabrication.

## Key Points

- TFLN enables fully integrated electronic-photonic circuits
- Key advantages: strong electro-optic interaction, low optical loss, high microwave bandwidth, scalable reconfigurability
- The platform uniquely integrates χ(2), χ(3), and electro-optic effects for exploring complex nonlinear dynamics
- Applications span: communications, computing, sensing, quantum information, photonic non-Hermitian synthetic dimensions, active topological physics, and quantum electro-optics
- Published May 2025, with >85 citations in some indices
