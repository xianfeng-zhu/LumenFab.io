## HyperLight (Cambridge, MA, USA)

**Status:** Commercial leader

- **Series B funding (Oct 2024):** $37M led by Summit Partners
- **Production line:** "Industry's first and only high-volume, qualified 6-inch TFLN manufacturing line" in production
- **8-inch pilot line:** Added March 2025 for future compatibility
- **Scale:** One 8-inch TFLN wafer holds >500 1.6T DR8 transmitter PICs
- **IP portfolio:** >70 patents pending and granted
- **TFLN Chiplet Platform:** Launched March 2025, targeting AI/datacom, telecom, test & measurement, quantum computing
- **Record product:** 110 GHz intensity modulator with record low Vπ (March 2025)
- **Quantum milestone (July 2025):** Partnered with Xanadu, achieved waveguide losses <2 dB/m and EO switch losses ~20 milli-dB
- **Customers:** Eoptolink uses HyperLight TFLN PICs
- **CEO:** Mian Zhang (co-inventor of the Nature 2018 breakthrough)

## Lightium (Switzerland)

**Status:** Early commercial, foundry model

- **Seed funding:** $7M led by Vsquared Ventures and Lakestar
- **Model:** Open-access TFLN foundry service
- **Production:** 200 mm (8-inch) wafers in high-volume foundry environment
- **PDK:** Available for C-band, O-band, 780 nm, 830 nm
- **Timeline:** Closed beta with strategic partners in 2024, public launch targeted early 2025
- **CEO:** Dr. Amir Ghadimi
- **Differentiation:** "First company to design and manufacture TFLN-based photonic chips at an industrial scale"

## CCRAFT / CSEM (Switzerland)

**Status:** EU open-access foundry, research to commercial transition

- **Origin:** Spinout from CSEM (April 2025), funded by EU ELENA project (€5M, 42 months)
- **Wafer size:** 150 mm (6-inch), progressing toward 200 mm (8-inch)
- **Wafer supplier:** Soitec (European supply chain)
- **PDK:** CLN600-Core (C-band) - first MPW closing Sep 2025; CLN600-Plus (C-band) - Dec 2025; O-band and Visible/Quantum planned
- **Waveguide performance:** 0.67 dB/cm for 0.8 µm waveguides; <0.28 dB/cm for wider waveguides
- **Capacity target:** Millions of TFLN chips annually (target 2028)
- **Building blocks:** Waveguides, MMLs, directional couplers, grating couplers, Y-splitters, high-speed modulators, phase shifters, nonlinear elements

## NanoLN (Jinan, China)

**Status:** Dominant commercial wafer supplier

- **Role:** Primary commercial LNOI wafer supplier (near-monopoly on raw wafers)
- **Product:** TFLN wafers (300 nm - 1 µm LN on SiO2/Si)
- **Standard size:** 4-inch (100 mm)
- **Significance:** Serving as the substrate source for most research groups and some foundries worldwide

## Other Players

- **SiPhotonIC:** Proprietary 400TFLN platform; MPW runs and on-demand fabrication; >60 GHz modulators; delivery 2.5 months standard
- **Luxtelligence (Europe):** Early stage, targeting MPW runs; several years from volume
- **AFR (Zhuhai, China):** Demonstrated 400 Gbps/lane TFLN modulator at OFC 2025
- **Ori-Chip:** Possibly second most advanced after HyperLight
