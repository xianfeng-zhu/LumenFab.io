# TFLN vs. SiPh vs. InP Modulator Benchmark Comparison

## Overview

This article presents a side-by-side comparison of four optical modulator platforms — bulk LiNbO3, InP (Indium Phosphide), SiPh (Silicon Photonics), and TFLN (Thin-Film Lithium Niobate) — across eight performance metrics.

## Key Takeaways

- TFLN earns "Excellent" on **6 of 8 metrics** (optical loss, bandwidth, half-wave voltage, extinction ratio, linearity, integration)
- Only "Median" ratings are chip length (cm-scale phase shifters vs. mm-scale for InP/SiPh) and cost (expected to decline with economies of scale)
- SiPh weakness: poor linearity due to free-carrier dispersion (nonlinear)
- TFLN is the only platform simultaneously offering >100 GHz bandwidth, <2V drive voltage, and athermal operation
