# CoPoS Research: Sources and References

## Academic Papers

1. **Seok, B.C. & Jung, J.P. (2024).** "Recent Progress of TGV Technology for High Performance Semiconductor Packaging." *J Weld Join*, 42(2), 155-164.
   - DOI: 10.5781/JWJ.2024.42.2.2
   - URL: https://www.e-jwj.org/journal/view.php?number=2032327&viewtype=pubreader
   - Covers: Glass substrates, TGV drilling (LISE, ECDM), functional layer coating, Cu electroplating, additive chemistry

2. **Lai, Y., Pan, K., & Park, S. (2024).** "Thermo-mechanical reliability of glass substrate and Through Glass Vias (TGV): A comprehensive review." *Microelectronics Reliability* (Elsevier).
   - Covers: Cu-metallized TGV reliability, thermal stress, crack mechanisms, adhesion layers

3. **Sun, P., Zhong, Y., Yu, D. et al. (2025).** "Recent Progress in Electroless Plating Process for Metallization of Through Glass Via." *Electronics and Packaging*, 25(7), 70107.
   - URL: https://m.researching.cn/articles/OJ445a31a8c9800e47
   - Covers: Electroless plating, adhesion enhancement, wet process metallization

4. **Zhao, J. et al. (2022).** "Stress Issues in 3D Interconnect Technology Using Through Glass Vias." *Journal of Mechanical Engineering*, 58(2), 246-258.
   - DOI: 10.3901/JME.2022.02.246
   - Covers: Thermal stress models, crack mechanisms, polymer stress relief

5. **Various (2026).** "Stress analysis of glass substrates vs. resin substrates in double-layer ABF sandwich structures." *MRS Advances*. Springer.
   - URL: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1557/s43580-026-01526-6

6. **Various (2025).** "Effect of tilted wall in through glass via on the electrochemical deposition process." *Electrochimica Acta*.
   - URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S001346862500917X

7. **Various (2024).** "SiO2 solid blocking layer on through-glass via surface for efficient highly selective copper filling." *Surfaces and Interfaces*.
   - URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2468023024015876

8. **Mosaic Microsystems (2022).** "Thin Glass: A Simplified Path to Copper-Filled Through-Glass Vias."
   - URL: https://www.3dincites.com/2022/11/thin-glass-a-simplified-path-to-copper-filled-through-glass-vias/
   - Viaffirm approach: temporary bonding, bottom-up Cu fill, debond
